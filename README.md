The burdened wanderer started out as a reskin but then things got changed and adjusted. She is plagued by a terrible affliction and is desperate to find a place to get the treatment she needs.

She'll be compatible with Py's extra cells mod and some secret cells still under heavy construction. When those release I'll also make her up to date for those mods.

When you build a certain cell, you'll get a notification, and that's when you can prove your worth as a true connoisseur of all things SQUISHY.

The questline uses base game stuff only so she is fine as a standalone mod.
She will however get worked into the Hathorverse and I want to make an alternate questline when other cells get released.
I'll also update the questline in the future where you get her in your Prison(inventory) as a quest reward. That feature is currently not yet supported so you have to go out and get her.

***V1.0 Released***
-2 Zip version attached. V1.0 and AtlasV1.0 so you can pick which to get. Atlas sheets demand less from your computer and the game so you can load more mods together if everyone uses atlas sheets in their mod.
If something doesn't show up correctly in your game, simple remove the atlas folder from the mod folder and you're good to go. If that sounds too complicated, just get the V1.0 version which uses the strips
folder and that will work.
